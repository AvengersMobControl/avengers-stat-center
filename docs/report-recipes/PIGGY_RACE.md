## Frozen field checklist, in priority order

1. Event date, event-time clan, number of participating unique players, their separate verified lobbies, complete-source footer and compared prior accepted Piggy date.
2. Four high-value headline blocks: TOTAL LINES, AVERAGE LINES / PARTICIPANT, CLAN-EARNED STARS with source-known count, and PLAYERS LOGGED / SPARKS context. Whole-lobby displayed POT STARS is a separate labeled reference, NEVER automatically substituted for clan-earned Stars. Show known vs missing reward counts. Rank **whole-lobby Big Pig pot Stars** against like-for-like historical weekly Big Pig pots of this same clan, showing rank and denominator when supported. Feature the **largest verified single individual pot** and recipient only; no 'top individual pot earners' leaderboard. Big Pig happens weekly, so never produce a weekday-only rank. Include meaningful percentage change in total lines and an average-lines-per-player trend over five comparable accepted Piggies when history supports it.
3. All returning-player **CAREER-WIDE** LINE PB improvements: player, actual previous Piggy line PB (including across transfers) and its date, current lines, absolute and percentage improvement. AV-2 `AV-KitKat` 14,379 on Oct23 is NOT a line PB because he recorded 17,137 in AVENGERS on Oct11; `AV-EventHorizon` 9,738 also is not a line PB because his old all-clan best is 14,000 on Sep13. First tracked appearance is not a new PB; do not invent an earlier baseline.
4. Independently qualified FINISHING-PLACE PBs: original game rank (not the rank by lines), previous qualifying rank and date, improved new rank. A qualifying AVENGERS lobby has at least 30 distinct AVENGERS members in THAT ONE lobby; AV-2 requires at least five AV-2 in THAT ONE lobby. Never add members across lobbies to meet the threshold or merge clan PB histories.
5. FIRST TRACKED clan Piggy appearances with name, lines and actual source rank; label first recorded, never first-ever globally. First tracked does not by itself mean newly joined.
6. NOTABLE NEW-MEMBER performances, independently: include name, lines, notable verified achievement, and membership timing/source. A newcomer may have historical data; never infer membership date from first appearance. If no supported new-member performances exist, say so in the fact pack rather than inventing one.
7. Top 10 by LINES: canonical account names and lines, not stars/sparks/share/efficiency columns. Top three can include source-backed prior career W / 2nd / 3rd / podium counts. Do not claim Top 10 are actual game-place 1–10 if lobby ranks differ.
8. Benchmarks for 5K+, then every 10K from 10K+ through 100K+ inclusive. The allowed tiers are 5K+, 10K+, 20K+, 30K+, 40K+, 50K+, 60K+, 70K+, 80K+, 90K+, 100K+. Count unique verified participating players with lines >= each threshold. **Display only tiers actually reached by at least one participant that week**, omitting every zero-count tier. A higher score qualifies for lower thresholds too. Gold only for a verified new or tied clan benchmark record. The graphic legend must read EXACTLY: GOLD = CLAN RECORD OR TIED RECORD.
9. AV-2-only RAKE %: sum the screenshot-displayed share percentages for every AV-2 participant in the SAME lobby, including participants outside the Top 10. Label as sum of displayed shares, not exact earned Stars / whole-pot ratio. Show AV-2 earned Stars and whole-lobby pot separately. If shares or lobby association are incomplete, mark unavailable and HOLD unattended publication rather than making up a rake.
